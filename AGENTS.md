# Project details

## Complaint & Maintenance Management
The system shall allow students to register a complaint related to hostel facilities.
The system shall allow students to optionally upload an image related to the complaint.
The system shall allow maintenance staff to view assigned complaints.
The system shall allow maintenance staff to update the complaint status (Open, In Progress, Resolved).
The system shall allow students to reopen a complaint if it is not satisfactorily resolved.
The system shall allow students to rate a complaint after resolution.
The system shall maintain a complete history of complaints for tracking and reporting.

## Hostel Announcements & Notices
The system shall allow administrators to create and publish hostel announcements.
Each announcement shall contain a title, content, posting date, and author.
The system shall allow students to view announcements within the platform.
The system shall support different types of notices such as water shutdowns, mess timing changes, and holiday notices.

## Mess Feedback & Meal Reporting
The system shall allow students to submit daily mess feedback.
The feedback shall include a rating and optional comments.
The system shall associate feedback with the student and date.
The system shall allow administrators to view aggregated reports including average ratings and total feedback count.
The system shall prevent multiple feedback submissions by the same student for the same date.
